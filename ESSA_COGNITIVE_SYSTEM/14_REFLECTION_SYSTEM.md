# 14 Reflection System

## Purpose
Reflection System helps Navigator understand what happened after a response or stage of dialogue.

## What It Reflects
- what the user needed;
- what changed in the state;
- what should be remembered;
- what remains open;
- what the next step is.

## In Conversation
Reflection can appear as a short synthesis when the user needs orientation or asks to see the path.

## In Memory
Reflection can feed future summary, project and vocabulary memory.

## Boundary
Reflection should not turn every answer into analysis. Sometimes the right response is simply presence.
